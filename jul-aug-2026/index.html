Summer 2026

Summer Recap: Air Shows, Flyouts, Races

Airspacers Board

It was a busy summer filled with fly-outs, meet-ups at airshows, participation in air races, and other events with old and new members. Here are a few highlights:

EAA AirVenture / Oshkosh

Oshkosh 2026 was an epic celebration of aviation and America’s 250th birthday. Several members were in attendance, including Matthew Setzer, Ryan Haid, Ian Dutton, Alex Silver, and Harry Sax. Harry made the ~30-hour round-trip flight from SMO in 4RM. The firework, drone, air, and pyrotechnics show on Wednesday night was one for the books.

COPA / CPPP Sacramento

A number of our Cirrus pilots attended the Cirrus Owners & Pilots Association in Sacramento, including Kris Maine, Aaron Weisblatt, Andrew Jackson, Alex Silver, and Oliver Graff. Part of our Cirrus fleet – 3GS and 47C – were in attendance as well.

Palms to Pines Air Race

The Palms to Pines Air Race is a 750-mile air race from Santa Monica to Central Oregon for female pilots, sponsored by the LA 99s. Members cheered on our very own Eve Lopez, who averaged over 200 knots GS, beating her handicap by 18 knots, and took home first place out of ~30 pilots. Congratulations Eve! 🥇

Around the Map

Ad Hoc Flyouts and Meetups

One of the best parts about Airspacers is unplanned, spontaneous fly-outs with fellow members. This summer, members flew everywhere from the Bay (SQL, PAO, MRY) to San Diego (CRQ, MYR) to Catalina (AVX) to mid-state (SBA, PRB, SBP) to Arizona (Sedona).

ℹ️ About Airspacers

Founded in 1958, Airspacers Flying Club is the oldest and most established flying club at Santa Monica Airport (KSMO), and one of the longest-running flying clubs in the greater LA area. Our mission has remained the same for over six decades: make the joy of aviation accessible, affordable, and community-driven.

We are a cooperative of 75 equity members, each an equal shareholder of Air-Spacers, Inc., the non-profit entity that owns and operates our aircraft, and an additional 30+ members across our Non-Equity, Social, and Leaseback membership tiers. Our fleet includes seven diverse airplanes, each suited for different styles and missions – available to any qualified member. To learn more, check out our website.

Have an idea for an article? Contact us. We’re interested in covering news within the Airspacers and broader Santa Monica aviation communities.
